Strategy Overview — What It Does

Cross-sectional mean reversion + momentum ensemble
We predict 5-day forward return for each stock in universe. We rank by predicted alpha, go long top 30% and short bottom 30% (or flat if you disable short).

Flow: Universe → Fetch yfinance OHLCV → Compute 20 Factors → LightGBM + XGB Ensemble → Alpha Rank → Kelly Sizing → Risk Checks → Paper Portfolio (next update)

Training uses walk-forward CV: train on 2y, validate on 3mo, roll forward. No look-ahead — features only use data up to t-1 to predict t+5.

Universe & Data

AAPL, MSFT, NVDA, GOOGL, AMZN, META, TSLA, SPY, QQQ, IWM + 20 more
Data: yfinance 1d OHLCV only. No alternative data. No hallucinated prices. History period: 5y max for training, 1mo-1y for live signals.

Source: yfinance Adj Close No Survivorship Bias Fix Yet

Alpha Model — Ensemble

LightGBM (60%) + XGBoost (40%)
Target: fwd_ret_5d = (Close[t+5]/Close[t] - 1) clipped to ±10% to reduce outlier impact.

LightGBM: 500 trees, max_depth 6, learning_rate 0.05, num_leaves 31, feature_fraction 0.8
XGBoost: 400 trees, max_depth 5, eta 0.05, subsample 0.8, colsample 0.8
Ensemble by averaging predictions, then z-scoring per day.

IC Target >0.03 CV Sharpe >1.2

Performance Philosophy

We don't chase returns, we chase consistency
Backtest includes: 0.05% commission per trade, slippage = 5bps, stop-loss 7%, take-profit 15%, max holding 20 days.

Metrics we care about: Hit Rate, Avg Win / Avg Loss, Max Drawdown, Vol-adjusted return. Not raw return.

20 Factors — Explained

All factors computed from OHLCV only, no external data. Each factor is cross-sectionally ranked per day to remove market drift.
ret_1d, ret_5d, ret_21d
Raw momentum — 1d, 1w, 1m returns. Captures trend.
mom_21_63
21d vs 63d momentum spread — medium term trend change.
dist_sma_20, dist_sma_50
Distance to SMA — mean reversion signal. Far from SMA = revert.
vol_ratio_20, vol_21d
Volume spike + volatility — high vol = institutional flow.
rsi_14, rsi_30
RSI — overbought >70, oversold <30 mean reversion.
bb_pos
Bollinger Band position — where price sits inside bands.
atr_14 / close
Normalized ATR — volatility regime filter.
high_20, low_20
Distance to 20d high/low — breakout vs breakdown.
corr_spy_21d
Correlation to SPY — high corr = market driven, low = idiosyncratic.
beta_63d
Beta to SPY — risk exposure.
skew_21d, kurt_21d
Return distribution shape — tail risk.
turnover_21d
Volume / shares out proxy — liquidity.
All factors z-scored per day across universe → model sees relative value, not absolute. No future data used.

Position Sizing & Risk

size = rank(alpha) × Kelly 0.35 × 12% cap
Step 1: Rank alphas 0-1, only trade top 30% (alpha > 70th percentile)
Step 2: Kelly = edge / odds, capped at 0.35 to avoid overbet
Step 3: Max position 12% of equity, max 8 positions, cash buffer 10%
Step 4: Vol targeting 15% annualized — if port vol >20%, scale down

Stop Loss 7% Take Profit 15% Max Hold 20d Fee 0.05%

Kill Switches

We stop trading when market is broken
VIX >30: Reduce size 50%, no new shorts
Drawdown >15%: Halt all new trades, only manage existing
SPY down >3% in day: No new longs
Model IC <0 for 20 days: Retrain signal, alert

All checks use yfinance live VIX (^VIX) and SPY.
