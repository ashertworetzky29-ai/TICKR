About — Why I Built TICKR

I'm Asher, 15, from Syracuse. I built TICKR because I wanted a portfolio tracker that doesn't hallucinate prices and a place to test my quant ideas without mixing them with my real holdings. Portfolio mode is 100% yfinance real data — every price, chart point, and news article is from Yahoo Finance, not AI generated. Quant mode is separate so it can break without breaking my portfolio. This is v14 — portfolio only. Paper trading and Quant Lab come next.

Metrics explained: Diversification = 100 - % in largest sector. Day Gain = sum(shares × today change). Chart = weighted sum of real closes × shares. SPY line normalized to your start.
